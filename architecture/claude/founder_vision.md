# Founder Vision — `mlai`

> The "why" behind the product, distilled from the original brief. This is the north star; when in doubt, decisions should serve this.

## The vision
Help investors **discover potential investment opportunities and build or challenge their investment conviction** using multiple sources of evidence in one place.

The transformation we enable:

**DATA → INFORMATION → CONTEXT → INSIGHT → OPPORTUNITY**

## Why this exists
Today's platforms are fragmented. Investors use one tool for charts, another for financials, another for news, another for screening — and must connect the pieces themselves. We connect them.

When a stock falls 25% in two weeks, a conventional screener just reports "-25%." We ask the questions that matter:
- *Why* did it fall? Did the business deteriorate, or was it temporary news?
- Was the whole sector affected? Did valuation compress? Was volume unusual?
- Has something similar happened before — and what happened after?
- What are the bull and bear cases? What should the investor monitor next?

This **contextual analysis** is the heart of the product.

## The founding insight
Real investing experiences shaped this:
- **Paytm (2024)** fell sharply. The question wasn't "should I buy the dip?" — it was whether the fall reflected genuine deterioration *or* a market overreaction while the long-term thesis stayed intact. It eventually recovered substantially.
- **Ola Electric** — a thesis that even if one part of a business struggles, other arms (e.g. battery manufacturing) could become long-term value drivers.

The lesson is **not** "buy stocks after they fall." It's that a large decline can be either **(A) genuine deterioration** or **(B) temporary overreaction** — and investors need tools to tell them apart. The platform helps *investigate and test* a thesis, not merely confirm it.

## What it is NOT
- Not an automatic trading system.
- Not a blind buy/sell/hold recommender.
- Not just another stock screener.
- Not a source of guaranteed returns.

## The promise to the user
We surface evidence and context; **the user makes the decision.** We help them build conviction when the evidence supports it — and challenge it when the evidence doesn't.
